03 / The shortlist method

Less guessing.
Better questions.

A useful Kakobuy shopping guide helps you evaluate a listing. Use these four steps to turn Kakobuy finds into a comparison you can actually act on.

  1. 01

    Choose the item, then the variant.

    Open the source listing and identify the exact color, size and version. Product links and thumbnail images may describe several different variants.

  2. 02

    Compare measurements with something you own.

    For T-shirt finds and jacket finds, check chest width, body length and sleeves. For pants finds and shorts finds, compare waist, rise and inseam.

  3. 03

    Inspect evidence and seller terms.

    Review materials, recent listing details and available QC photos. Check return conditions before ordering. Product photo checks cannot establish authenticity.

  4. 04

    Compare the complete estimated cost.

    Include applicable delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency charges and import costs. Leave room for charges that are not yet confirmed.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a curated product list used by shoppers researching items for purchase through Kakobuy. It may organize product links, categories, listed prices and notes. Entries are starting points for research, not guarantees of availability, quality or authenticity.

Is this an official Kakobuy website or a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent browsing guide with links to CAFANS categories. It is not operated by Kakobuy and does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet or a live Kakobuy inventory feed.

How do I use a Kakobuy spreadsheet for shoes and clothing?

Choose a category, shortlist relevant items and check the original listing. Compare measurements, materials, available variants, seller terms and the estimated total cost before deciding whether to order.

Do QC photos prove that a branded item is authentic?

No. Quality-control photos can help you inspect visible details, but they do not establish authenticity. For branded purchases, seek verifiable provenance and buy from the brand or an authorized retailer when authenticity is essential.

What costs should I compare beyond the product price?

Consider applicable domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion charges, taxes and import duties. Estimates depend on the destination, parcel details and services used.

Are the illustrated items confirmed CAFANS listings?

No. The brand-themed graphics are editorial illustrations, not product photographs or verified stock listings. Follow a category link to inspect current listings and their individual details.
